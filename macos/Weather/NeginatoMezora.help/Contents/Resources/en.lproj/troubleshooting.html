Troubleshooting & FAQ

The forecast says "Stale" or "Couldn't load the forecast"

The last refresh failed; hover over the Stale badge for the reason. The app retries automatically (every 10 minutes, and when the window comes back to the front). If only some services failed, the page says Not available right now: … and the forecast is blended from the others. ⌘R retries now (not more often than every 10 seconds).

Refresh does nothing

Refresh is ignored while the place is loading, within 10 seconds of its last refresh, for 60 seconds after a service asked for fewer requests, and while offline. Forecasts are also not fetched while the window is hidden or minimised.

The footer shows no station, or only a distant one

No station within 95 km reported in the last 3 hours, or the nearest ones are far. In the UK, the Netherlands, Norway, France and Spain, a free key adds the national network (Free Keys for More Stations). Settings › Stations › Stations Near… shows which stations are known for the selected place and their state.

A station near me reads clearly wrong

Each station is tested against its neighbours at every refresh; one that is off by more than 1.5 °C loses weight, from 8 °C it is not used, and a stuck reading is set aside after 8 hours. A brand-new station starts at 60% weight. If you have reason to distrust a whole network, turn it off in Settings › Stations.

The key I entered "was refused"

  • Paste it again: the whole key, without extra characters (spaces at the ends are removed automatically).
  • Check that the key is for the right product of the service; some services only activate a key after you confirm your e-mail address or subscribe it to the observations product.
  • Some keys expire (AEMET's after 3 months); create a new one on the service's site and use Replace….

Each service's specific causes are listed in When a key does not work.

"The service's limit for this key was reached"

The free plan's request limit was hit, usually the Met Office's 360 calls a day with many UK places. The app keeps the network's recent readings for up to 90 minutes and tries again at the next refresh; the Met Office count resets at 00:00 UTC.

The forecast changed after I added stations or a key

More or closer stations change the "now" values, and over a few days the learned local corrections. That is expected. To compare with the plain models, turn off Correct forecasts with what nearby stations measure in Settings › Stations.

Learned corrections look wrong

Corrections are capped at ±4 °C and need several readings per hour of day, but a place whose only close station is badly sited can teach the wrong lesson. Reset the place in Settings › Stations › Learned Local Corrections, and consider turning off that station's network.

Why does the temperature differ from my phone's weather app?

Most weather apps show a single model or a provider's own blend for a grid point. Neginato Mezora blends several models, grounds "now" in tested nearby stations, and corrects for the place's learned local bias, so it can differ by a few degrees, especially at night, in valleys and near water.

The air quality number seems off, or shows a dash

The small line on the card says where the number comes from. A measured value is the nearest AirNow monitor's latest hourly reading, as reported; the monitor can be up to 45 km away, in a different setting (for example next to a busy road), and AirNow's data are preliminary. A CAMS model forecast describes an area 11–45 km wide and can miss local sources such as traffic, wood smoke or nearby wildfire smoke. A dash means neither source has answered yet; offline the card needs the network. See Reading the Forecast. For official alerts, follow your local air-quality agency.

I want to start over

Reset the learned corrections and forget the stations in Settings › Stations; reset weights in Settings › Weights. To erase everything, see Privacy, Data & Storage.
