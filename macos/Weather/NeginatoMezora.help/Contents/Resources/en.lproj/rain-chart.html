Rain Graph & Bars Guide

The Rain card in the detail pane displays up to two charts side-by-side: Next 2 hours (15-minute intervals, shown across the US, Canada, UK, Netherlands, Germany, and Europe when sub-hourly nowcast data is available) and Next 24 hours (hourly intervals). Each visual series is explicitly labeled in the header legend and on the axes.

Bars = Rain Rate (Intensity, Left Axis)
The vertical aqua-to-cobalt bars show how hard it is expected to rain in in/h (Imperial) or mm/h (Metric). Read bar heights against the Left Y-Axis (labeled Rate · 0.10 in/h / 2.5 mm/h at the top, half-scale at the middle dashed line, and 0 at the baseline).
Curve = Rain Chance (Probability %, Right Axis)
On the 24-hour chart, the smooth amber-rose curve with glowing circular nodes shows how likely measurable rain (≥ 0.1 mm/h) is during each hour, from 0% to 100%. Read the curve against the Right Y-Axis (labeled 100% at top, 50% at midline, and 0% at bottom).

Reading On-Chart Callouts & Hover Inspection

  • Peak Bar Badges: Up to two highest non-zero bars display a cyan badge directly above the bar cap showing the exact precipitation rate (e.g., 0.08 in/h or 1.5 mm/h).
  • Peak Chance Badge: The highest probability hour on the 24-hour curve displays an amber-rose badge (e.g., Peak 80%).
  • Interactive Hover Readout: Move the pointer across any time slot on either chart to reveal a vertical guide line and a floating summary banner at the top of the plot: 3:00 PM · Bar: 0.06 in/h · Chance: 70%.
