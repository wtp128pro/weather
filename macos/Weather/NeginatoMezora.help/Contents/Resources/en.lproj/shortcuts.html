⌘NOpen Add a City / Street Address / Pin window

Adding Custom Locations, Street Addresses & Map Pins

Press ⌘N or click the + button in the toolbar to open the location picker:

  • Search Tab: Enter any city name, street address, postal code, landmark, or raw latitude, longitude pair. Neginato Mezora queries Apple MapKit, US Census TIGER/Line, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, OSM Photon, and Open-Meteo in parallel.
  • Pin on Map Tab: Click anywhere on the interactive Apple Map (or enter exact coordinates and a custom label) to save a hyper-local pin.
