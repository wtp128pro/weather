Live Station 3D Assimilation

To capture local microclimates (sea breezes, valley cold pools, urban heat islands, and local gust fronts), LiveStationFetcher.swift assimilates live physical observations from nearby airport METAR stations, NWS ASOS stations, DWD/KNMI 10-minute SYNOP stations via Bright Sky, 90m DEM surface analysis, and US EPA AirNow + Copernicus CAMS/EEA air quality networks.

1. 3D Anisotropic Spatial Kernel

Each physical station at horizontal distance d (km) and elevation difference Δz (m) is weighted by:

W_horiz(d) = exp(-d² / (2 * 22.0²)) / (1 + (d / 8.0)²)
W_vert(Δz) = exp(-Δz² / (2 * 350.0²))
W_spatial  = W_horiz(d) * W_vert(Δz)

2. Diurnal Lapse-Rate Elevation Reduction

Station temperatures and dewpoints are reduced to the target location's elevation using boundary-layer lapse rates before blending:

  • Daytime Dry-Bulb Lapse Rate: Γ_T = -0.0068 °C/m (6.8 °C/km mixed convective boundary layer)
  • Nighttime Dry-Bulb Lapse Rate: Γ_T = -0.0042 °C/m (4.2 °C/km nocturnal inversion damping)
  • Dewpoint Lapse Rate: Γ_Td = -0.0019 °C/m

3. Sub-Hourly National Station Networks & Air Quality

  • US NWS ASOS & Ceilometer Fusion: Queries api.weather.gov/stations/{id}/observations/latest for 5-minute ASOS updates and laser-ceilometer cloud layers.
  • Germany & Netherlands 10-Minute SYNOP: Queries api.brightsky.dev/current_weather for 10-minute DWD and KNMI surface observations across Central Europe and Benelux.
  • Regional Air Quality (EPA AirNow + Copernicus CAMS/EEA): Uses inverse-square distance weighting across local EPA AirNow monitors in North America and Copernicus CAMS / European Environment Agency (EAQI, PM2.5, NO₂) in Europe.

4. Shepard Directional Isolation & MAD Buddy QC

  • Angular De-Clustering (Shepard, 1968): Stations clustered along the same compass bearing from the target share angular shadow penalty W_dir = 1 / (1 + shadow).
  • Variable-Specific Temporal Decay: Exponential decay time constants τ_temp = 2700 s (45 min), τ_wind = 1200 s (20 min), and τ_wx = 900 s (15 min).
  • MAD Outlier Quality Control (Rousseeuw & Croux, 1993): When ≥ 3 stations report, any sensor deviating by more than 3.2 × MAD is penalized by a quadratic Huber tail weight.
  • 6-Hour MOS Bias Decay (Glahn & Lowry, 1972): The observed temperature innovation ΔT_0 decays across the first 6 forecast hours via ΔT(t) = ΔT_0 * exp(-Δt / 3.5h).
