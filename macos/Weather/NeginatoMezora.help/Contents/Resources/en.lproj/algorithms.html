Ensemble Blending & Meteorological Citations

Every forecast value in Neginato Mezora is computed by deterministic, unit-tested algorithms in WeatherCore.

1. IEEE-754 frexp/ldexp Weighted Median (WeightedMedian.swift)

Continuous variables (temperature, feels-like, wind speed, gusts, cloud cover, relative humidity, and precipitation amount) are blended using the weighted median (Edgeworth, 1888) rather than an arithmetic mean so a single outlier model cannot skew the forecast. To prevent IEEE-754 floating-point overflow or underflow without altering mantissa bits, weights are rescaled by an exact power of two via frexp and ldexp (Goldberg, 1991):

w_tilde_i = ldexp(w_i, -max_exp)   // exact exponent shift, zero mantissa rounding

When cumulative weight lands strictly on the 50% midpoint, the two adjacent values are averaged using an overflow-safe sign-aware formula (meanOfPair).

2. Mass-Conserving 15-Minute Nowcast Rescaling (Nowcast.swift)

Across the US, Canada, UK, Netherlands, Germany, and Europe, 15-minute sub-hourly precipitation quarters (from NOAA HRRR, UKMO 2 km, KNMI HARMONIE 2 km, or DWD ICON-D2 2 km) are summed into full UTC hours prior to blending. After multi-model blending produces the consensus hourly total B_H, the 3 smaller quarters scale by B_H / raw_sum and the first largest quarter absorbs the exact remainder B_H - rest so the 4 quarters sum identically to B_H.

3. Multi-Model Precipitation Probability Consensus (Blend.swift)

When explicit precipitation probability models represent a minority of the active precipitation ensemble (for example, when regional NWP models report hourly rain rate precipMm without an explicit probability field), WxBlend combines the reported probability mean with the weighted fraction of ensemble members predicting measurable precipitation (precipMm ≥ 0.1 mm/h).

4. WMO Code 4677 & Hydrometeor Classification (ConditionRules.swift)

  • Thunderstorm Majority: ≥ 50% of WMO-reporting members in {95, 96, 97, 99}.
  • Snow Majority: ≥ 50% in {71, 73, 75, 77, 85, 86} and blended temperature T ≤ 2.0 °C.
  • Sleet / Freezing Rain: ≥ 50% in {56, 57, 66, 67}, or wet hour with 0.0 °C ≤ T < 1.5 °C.
  • Wet Hour Criterion: precipMm ≥ 0.1 mm/h and precipProb ≥ 30% (classified as .showers when cloudPct < 87.5% and precipMm < 7.6 mm/h, else steady .rain).
