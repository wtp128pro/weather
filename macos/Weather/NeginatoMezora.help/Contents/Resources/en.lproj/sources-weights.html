Settings: Data Sources & Weights

Open the Neginato Mezora Settings dialogue at any time by pressing ⌘,, clicking the gear (⚙) button in the top-right window toolbar, or choosing Neginato Mezora > Settings… from the menu bar.

Tab 1: Sources

The Sources tab controls which upstream weather providers participate in forecast refreshes and sets each provider's overall blend weight multiplier:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo): Supplies up to 9 NWP models (NOAA HRRR 3 km + 15-min Nowcast, NOAA NBM, ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON, ECCC GEM, UK Met Office, Météo-France, and KNMI HARMONIE-AROME 2 km). Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • National Weather Service (nws): Official NOAA human-forecaster 2.5 km NDFD grid for the United States, Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands, Guam, and American Samoa. US Government public domain work.
  • MET Norway (met-no): Norwegian Meteorological Institute Locationforecast 2.0 (1 km Nordic MEPS + global ECMWF IFS). Licensed under CC BY 4.0 / NLOD.
  • DWD Bright Sky (dwd-brightsky): Deutscher Wetterdienst MOSMIX station-calibrated guidance + ICON-D2 / SYNOP observations for Germany, the Netherlands, Benelux, and Central Europe. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • Environment Canada (eccc): Meteorological Service of Canada GeoMet Official Citypage & HRDPS 2.5 km guidance for Canada. Licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.

Each source row includes a Source weight multiplier slider (0.10× to 3.00×, default 1.00×) that scales all models belonging to that provider.

Tab 2: Weights & Model Matrix

The Weights tab lets you choose a regional profile (Inspect Regional Profile) and variable group (Temperature, Wind, Cloud Cover, Humidity, Precipitation Amount, Precipitation Chance) to inspect the 4-band horizon weights (H0: 0–3h, H1: 3–18h, H2: 18–72h, H3: 72h+) and adjust individual multipliers (0.00× to 3.00×) for every NWP model.

Baseline World Weights (Rest of World)

In .world, US-only models (nws, hrrr, nbm) receive weight 0.0. Global and regional models use baseline weights: open-meteo:ecmwf = 3.0, open-meteo:icon = 2.5, open-meteo:ukmo = 2.0, open-meteo:meteofrance = 2.0, open-meteo:knmi = 2.0, open-meteo:gem = 1.5, open-meteo:gfs = 1.5, met-no:ecmwf = 1.5, dwd-brightsky:mosmix = 3.0, and eccc:hrdps = 3.5.
