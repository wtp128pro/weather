Settings: Data Sources & Weights

Open Settings with ⌘, (it opens on the Sources tab), the gear button in the toolbar, or Neginato Mezora › Settings…. Neginato Mezora › Data Sources & Weights… opens the Weights tab directly.

The Sources tab

One row per forecast service. Each row has a status dot and line, an on/off switch, a Source weight multiplier, and links to the service's credit and licence.

greyNot used for this placeThe service does not cover the selected place (for example the NWS outside the US).
greyTurned offYou switched it off. It is not fetched for any place.

Switches and source multipliers

  • The switch turns a service off for every place. Every place is fetched again, without it.
  • The Source weight multiplier (0.10× to 3.00×, steps of 0.05, default 1.00×) scales the weight of all of that service's models. 1.0× resets one row; Reset Source Weights (in the section header, shown when any is changed) resets them all.
  • The note under the list is meant literally: the weights are rules of thumb, not statistics measured from past accuracy, so the app claims no accuracy figure.

Station networks are not in this tab: they have their own switches in Settings › Stations. Nor are the official alert sources: they have no switch and need no key; they and their credits are on Weather Alerts.

The Weights tab

Every forecast hour is a weighted median of the models that cover it (see Algorithms). A model's weight depends on the regional profile of the place, the variable, and how far ahead the hour is, in four horizon bands: H0 0–3 hours, H1 3–18 hours, H2 18–72 hours, H3 beyond 72 hours.

  • Inspect Regional Profile shows the weights of one profile. It starts on the profile of the place selected in the main window, and follows it when you select another place; it only changes what you see, not which profile a place uses.
  • Inspect Baseline Variable Group: Temperature & Feels Like, Wind & Gusts, Cloud Cover, Relative Humidity, Precipitation Amount, Precipitation Chance.
  • Each model row shows its name and id, where it runs, its resolution, the four band weights of the chosen profile and variable, the worldwide baseline, and a Model multiplier (0.00× to 3.00×; 0 removes the model from the blend). 1.0× resets one model; Reset All to 1.00× resets every multiplier.
  • The weight used is profile weight × source multiplier × model multiplier. Multipliers apply in every region. Changing one re-fetches every place.
