Reading the Forecast

Select a place in the sidebar to see its forecast. From top to bottom: the header, Now, Alerts, Rain, Air Quality and Clouds side by side, Next 48 hours, Next days, the footer, the credits, and (when a source failed) a Not available right now line. The background colour follows the current sky, day or night. All times are in the place's own time zone.

Header

The place name with its region (or your Location Name with its address line), and a Name Location / Edit Name button (see Naming a place).

Now

  • The condition icon, the temperature (for example 77°F) and the condition: Clear, Mostly clear, Partly cloudy, Mostly cloudy, Overcast, Fog, Drizzle, Light rain, Rain, Heavy rain, Showers, Thunderstorm, Snow or Sleet.
  • Feels like: wind chill when it is cold and windy, heat index when it is hot and humid, otherwise the temperature.
  • High · Low of today. When the forecast only covers the rest of today, it says so, for example (from 2 PM). If the live temperature is already outside the forecast range, the range is widened to include it.
  • Wind (with gusts when they are reported meaningfully above the mean wind) and Humidity.
Where "now" comes from
When stations are near, the Now values are a blend of the live station readings (tested and weighted; see Live Station Observations) and a high-resolution model analysis for the exact spot. The closer and fresher the stations, the more they count: about 95% for a station next door, about 75% at 25 km, about 40% at 60 km. Without stations the model values are shown. The footer names the nearest station.

Alerts

The official warnings in force for the place, one card each, highest level first; or one line saying there are none or why none can be shown. The card is also shown while the forecast is loading or could not be loaded; that page scrolls when the alerts do not fit. See Weather Alerts.

Rain

A headline and a detail line, then one or two charts.

  • Headlines include No rain expected for the next 48 hours, Raining now (with easing soon, easing around 4 PM or expected to continue for hours), Rain starting in 25 minutes, Rain expected tomorrow around 4 PM, and Showers possible … (40% chance).
  • Detail line: how heavy (Very light below 0.5 mm/h, Light below 2.5, Moderate below 7.6, Heavy above) and when it peaks, with the chance of rain, or No steady rain in the forecast, just a chance.
  • Live: if the forecast says dry but a nearby station reports precipitation, the headline says so (for example Light rain reported now, with the station and its rate). If the 15-minute data shows rain starting within 90 minutes, the headline gives the minutes.
  • Charts: Next 2 hours (15-minute bars, when available) and Next 24 hours (hourly bars and a chance curve). See the Rain Graph & Bars Guide.

Air Quality

The US Air Quality Index (AQI, 0–500) with its category, on a scale of six equal bands in the official EPA colours: Good (0–50, green), Moderate (51–100, yellow), Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (101–150, orange), Unhealthy (151–200, red), Very Unhealthy (201–300, purple) and Hazardous (301–500, maroon). The marker always sits in the band of its category. Below the scale:

  • A comparison with yesterday: the same source's value 24 hours earlier, for example Air quality index is 44, similar to yesterday at this time (37). Within 8 points it is similar; otherwise better (lower) or worse (higher). Without a value from yesterday the sentence gives the category instead (which is good).
  • Where the value comes from, in a smaller line.

Clouds

The cloud cover now (for example Cloud cover 40% · Partly cloudy) with a gauge, and how it will change (Partly cloudy now, clearing around 4 PM, Clear skies for the next 24 hours). Bands: Clear below 12.5%, Mostly clear below 37.5%, Partly cloudy below 62.5%, Mostly cloudy below 87.5%, Overcast above. With live stations, high thin cloud counts less than low thick cloud (what you actually see), and the sentence names the observing station.

Next 48 hours

One card per hour, scrolling sideways: the hour (Now for the current one), icon, temperature, rain chance and cloud cover. Rain chances of 30% or more, and wet hours, are highlighted. Hover for feels-like, wind and the rain amount. Wet hours near freezing show snow or sleet icons. With live stations, the Now card shows the live values, and the next 6 hours ease from the observed temperature into the forecast.

Next days

Up to 10 days: Today, Tomorrow, then weekdays. Each row has the day's icon, its chance of rain, the low, a range bar and the high. The bars share one scale, so you can compare days at a glance; a white dot marks the current temperature on today's bar. Hover for the rain amount and cloud cover; a day only partly covered by the forecast says so.

Footer, badges and credits

  • Updated 5 min ago · Sources: … lists the services that answered. With stations: · Station: KMIA (3.2 mi) +4 nearby, the nearest station and how many more were blended.
  • A spinner shows while the place refreshes. Offline: no network, showing the last forecast. Stale: the last refresh failed (hover for the reason); the previous forecast is shown.
  • The credits line links every forecast service that answered, every station network whose readings are shown, and the air-quality source, as their licences require. Networks whose licence asks for the date of the data (Météo-France, AEMET) show the time of their newest report, for example Source: AEMET, data of 25 Oct 14:00 UTC.
  • Not available right now: … names services that failed this time; the forecast is blended from the others.

When learned local corrections are on, the temperatures on this page (hours, days, highs and lows) include them. See Learned Local Corrections.
