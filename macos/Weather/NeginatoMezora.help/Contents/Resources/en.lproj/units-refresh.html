Units, Refreshing & Offline

Units

Every place has its own units, Imperial (°F, mph, inches) or Metric (°C, km/h, mm), saved with the place. The sidebar shows each place's temperature with its unit, and the forecast page uses the units of the place it shows.

  • When you add a place, the units menu of the Add a city window (Units for and the place) sets them, preselected from the place's country (see Adding a place).
  • To switch a saved place, select it and click the unit button in the toolbar (it shows the selected place's unit, °F or °C) or choose View › Switch Selected Place to Celsius (or to Fahrenheit; ⌘U), the title the button's tooltip shows too. Only the selected place changes, at once; its forecast is kept, not fetched again.
  • With no place selected (for example with an empty list), the button and ⌘U, then titled Set Default Units to Celsius (or to Fahrenheit), switch the default units for new places instead.
  • Settings › General › Default Units for New Places is used for a new place whose country is not known. Places you have already added keep their own units.
  • Settings › Stations shows its values in the selected place's units (the default units when none is selected).

Temperatures, wind, rain amounts and rates, the rain chart scale, and station and monitor distances (mi or km) change; percentages and the air quality index do not. Times are shown in 12-hour form in each place's time zone.

When forecasts refresh

  • Forecasts are fetched only while the window is visible (on screen, not minimised or hidden) and the Mac is online.
  • When the window appears, comes back to the front, or the network returns, places whose forecast is at least 2 minutes old are refreshed.
  • Every 10 minutes, places whose forecast is at least 9 minutes old are refreshed. At most 3 places are fetched at once, the selected place first.
  • ⌘R (View › Refresh, or the refresh button) refreshes the selected place now. It does nothing while that place is loading, within 10 seconds of its last refresh, for 60 seconds after a service asked for fewer requests, or while offline.
  • Changing a source switch or a weight re-fetches every place once.
  • A refresh waits up to 30 seconds for the services; one that has not answered by then is listed as Not available right now. Network errors are retried twice.
  • Official alerts are checked after each completed forecast refresh of a place, never on their own timer and never while offline. How often each alert source is actually asked is on Weather Alerts.

Live stations and air quality

  • Live station readings and air quality are fetched with each forecast refresh of a place, and again for every place when a station setting (a key, a network switch) changes.
  • Answers are kept for a short time and shared by places close together: 2–10 minutes for most station networks, 15 minutes for AEMET, an hour per Met Office station (its free plan allows 360 calls a day); station lists are kept for a day (the Met Office's list of nearest stations for a week). Air quality: monitors 10 minutes, yesterday's monitor readings an hour, the CAMS forecast 15 minutes.
  • A network that cannot be reached contributes its readings of the last 90 minutes.

Offline

When the Mac loses its connection, rows show Offline, the forecast page shows the Offline badge and the last forecast (without live station values or air quality), and refreshing is off. Alerts are not checked, and alert answers are not kept between launches. A place with an alert answer (alerts or No active alerts) reads Alerts could not be checked recently. once that answer is older than its source's window; a place with no answer yet keeps Checking for alerts…, one whose last check failed keeps Alerts could not be checked right now., and one no source covers keeps Alerts not available. As soon as the connection is back, places are refreshed. At launch, each place shows its last forecast from the cache with its age until the new one arrives.

Saving and quitting

Changes are saved a few seconds after you make them. Quitting (or closing the window, which quits) waits up to 5 seconds for pending saves.
