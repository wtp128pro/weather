Welcome to Neginato Mezora

Neginato Mezora is a native macOS meteorological workstation and hyper-local precipitation nowcasting application. It synthesizes up to 10 numerical weather prediction (NWP) models from three free upstream providers (Open-Meteo, NOAA National Weather Service, and MET Norway) and fuses them with real-time physical airport METARs, 90m DEM surface analysis, and US EPA AirNow air quality monitors.

Quick Navigation

  • Settings: Data Sources & Weights (⌘,) — Configure enabled providers and adjust per-source and per-model blend weights.

  • Live Station 3D Assimilation — How nearby physical weather stations and air quality sensors are fused in real time.
