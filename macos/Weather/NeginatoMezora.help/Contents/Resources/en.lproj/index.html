Welcome to Neginato Mezora

Neginato Mezora shows the weather for the places you save, with an emphasis on accuracy and on when it will rain. Every forecast is a blend of up to 13 forecast models from five free services (Open-Meteo, the US National Weather Service, MET Norway, the German weather service DWD via Bright Sky, and Environment Canada). The current conditions are grounded in live readings of nearby weather stations, and the forecast learns each place's local quirks from those stations.

Start here
  1. Add a place: press ⌘N (or the + button) and type a city, street address, ZIP or postal code, landmark, or coordinates. See Adding & Managing Places.
  2. Read the forecast: select the place in the sidebar. The detail pane shows now, rain, air quality, clouds, the next 48 hours and the next days. See Reading the Forecast.
  3. Get more stations (optional): in the UK, the Netherlands, Norway, France and Spain, a free key from the national weather service adds its stations. See Free Keys for More Stations.

What the app does for accuracy

LayerWhat it doesLearn more
Model blendUp to 13 forecast models are combined hour by hour with a weighted median, with weights that depend on the region and on how far ahead the hour is (high-resolution national models lead the first hours; the best global models lead later).Data Sources & Weights, Algorithms
15-minute rainWhere a 15-minute model is available (US, Canada, UK, Netherlands, Germany and much of Europe), the next 2 hours of rain are shown in 15-minute steps.Rain Graph & Bars
Live stationsEvery station network that covers the place is read at each refresh (airports worldwide plus national, road-weather and research networks), the stations are tested against each other, and the trusted ones are blended into the "now" values.Live Station Observations
Learned local correctionsFor each place and hour of the day, the app learns how far the models run from what close stations measure, and corrects the forecast temperatures, feels-like, highs and lows by up to ±4 °C (±7 °F).Learned Local Corrections
Air qualityThe nearest air-quality monitor's reading as reported (US and Canada), otherwise the CAMS air-quality forecast. Never estimated from the weather.Reading the Forecast

Where things are

  • Sidebar: your saved places (up to 50), each with its temperature and a rain hint. Drag to reorder; the … button names, moves or removes a place.
  • Toolbar: + (add a place), refresh, the unit button (°F/°C), and the gear (Settings).
  • Settings (⌘,): four tabs: Sources, Weights, Stations and General. The app menu also has Data Sources & Weights… and Stations & Keys…, which open those tabs directly.
  • Help (⌘?): this book. The search field at the top left searches every page, and the Help menu opens the main pages directly.
