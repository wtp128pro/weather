Settings: Stations

Open Settings (⌘,) and choose Stations, or use Neginato Mezora › Stations & Keys…. The tab has four sections; the Help links in their headers open the matching help pages.

1. Learned Local Corrections

  • Correct forecasts with what nearby stations measure turns the corrections on or off for every place. When off, the forecasts show the plain model blend; learning continues, so turning it back on needs no new learning.
  • One row per place, with its status, for example 7 of 24 hours learned from 19 readings, last 12 min ago, or Nothing learned yet: this needs station readings within about 25 km that are less than 45 minutes old. A place without updates for over 30 days adds fading: no new readings for N days.
  • Now: the correction applied at this moment (for example −1.2 °F: the forecast is lowered by 1.2 °F).
  • The bars: one per local hour of today. Up (orange) means the forecast is raised at that hour, down (blue) lowered. A small dot marks an hour with readings but not yet enough to correct it. The current hour is highlighted; hover over a bar for its value and number of readings.
  • Reset forgets one place's learning; Reset All… forgets every place's (after a confirmation).

How the learning works: Learned Local Corrections.

2. Networks That Need Your Free Key

Met Office (UK), KNMI (Netherlands), MET Norway Frost (Norway), Météo-France (France) and AEMET (Spain). For each one:

  • Paste your key into the field and click Save (or press Return). The key is stored in your login Keychain, not in a file. Once saved, the row shows … saved in your Keychain with Replace… and Remove. For AEMET and Météo-France keys, which carry an expiry date, the row shows it, and warns when it is near or past.
  • How to get a free … opens the steps for that service, with a button to its sign-up page. The full walk-through, with limits and troubleshooting, is in Free Keys for More Stations.
  • The switch turns the network off without removing the key.
  • The status line and dot tell you whether the key works (see below).

3. Networks Used Without a Key

Airport reports (worldwide), NWS airport stations and the Iowa Environmental Mesonet (US), Environment Canada stations, Deutscher Wetterdienst, DMI, FMI, SMHI, GeoSphere Austria and MeteoSwiss. Each has a switch: a network turned off is neither fetched nor used. Each row links the network's credit.

4. Stations Near the Selected Place

The stations within 60 km of the place selected in the main window, nearest first (12 shown; Show All for the rest). Each row gives the station's name and code, network, distance, the age of its latest report, its weight (0–100%) and why:

StateMeaning
Good (green)Agrees with its neighbours within 1.5 °C (2.7 °F): full weight.
On probation (N of 6 tests) (blue)New or returning: weight rises from 60% to 100% over its first 6 tests.
Reduced weight (off by X) (yellow)Its typical error is above 1.5 °C; the larger the error, the lower the weight.
Not used: off by X (red)Error of 8 °C (14 °F) or more. It keeps being tested and can recover.
Not used: reading stuck (red)The reading has not changed for 8 hours (a failed sensor). Used again as soon as it changes.

Forget All Stations… clears the station list and every track record (after a confirmation). The stations are found again at the next refresh and start over on probation; learned local corrections are kept.
